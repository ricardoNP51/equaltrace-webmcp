# EqualTrace B3 final video plan

Date: 2026-08-30 (America/La_Paz)
Mode: autonomous straight run
Target: final presenter-free Devpost video candidate

## Decision

B3 keeps V2's forensic/editorial identity and frozen public-release evidence, but changes the opening from technical explanation to a concrete human loss:

> A person saw the consequences, gave exact consent, and received a recovery path. The agent deleted the same account without four of those protections.

The video must create tension before explaining architecture. The judge should understand the conflict before learning the terms "protection parity" or "dynamic capability".

## Success criteria

- The concrete account-deletion conflict is understandable in the first 6 seconds.
- The `6/6 · 6/6 · 2/6` contrast appears within the first 10 seconds.
- The first divergence is visible before 25 seconds.
- Human authority is framed as the person regaining control, not as a feature list.
- The native `4 → 5 → 4` surface remains explicit and accurately labelled.
- The repaired rerun and deterministic receipt remain supported by frozen evidence.
- Every spoken line has a synchronized on-screen caption.
- Runtime stays below 1:45, with no presenter face or participant voice.
- The title lockup includes the comprehensible hook: `The agent succeeded. The safety contract failed.`
- The render passes 1920×1080, H.264/AAC, loudness, peak, duration, and visual contact-sheet review.

## Storyboard

1. **0:00–0:08 — Human stakes.** Show the four protections a person received, then remove them from the agent route.
2. **0:08–0:18 — The impossible-to-ignore gap.** Put `Visual 6/6`, `Assistive 6/6`, and `Agent 2/6` on one frame with the same deleted outcome.
3. **0:18–0:32 — First break, not final state.** Show the exact missing `disclosure.consequences` evidence.
4. **0:32–0:48 — Authority returns to a person.** The agent proposes; a person reviews the exact action, consequence, digest, seed, and expiry.
5. **0:48–1:06 — Native WebMCP payoff.** Show the repair tool as absent, available once, and removed: `4 → 5 → 4`.
6. **1:06–1:24 — Proof, not promise.** Fresh three-route rerun reaches `6/6` and the same receipt across five native runs.
7. **1:24–1:36 — Brand close.** `EqualTrace` plus the hook and the invariant: no route gets fewer protections.

## Admission and honesty rules

- Reuse only the four frozen public-release screenshots and recorded release numbers.
- Do not depict the explanatory animation as native evidence.
- Do not introduce generated people, stock footage, external APIs, or a new product claim.
- Do not replace technical proof with emotion; emotion opens the story and evidence closes it.
- Preserve V2 as a comparison artifact and render B3 to a new filename.
