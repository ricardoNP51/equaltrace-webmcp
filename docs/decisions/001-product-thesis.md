# ADR 001: Protection parity is the product thesis

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-08-26

## Decision

EqualTrace will compare semantic protections across visual, assistive, and WebMCP routes for the same consequential action.

## Why

A broad accessibility auditor overlaps established tools and weakens the WebMCP-specific argument. Outcome-only verification overlaps contract and agent evaluation products. Protection parity addresses a concrete WebMCP risk while remaining understandable to non-specialist judges.

## Consequences

- The golden scenario stays narrow and consequential.
- Every feature must strengthen capture, comparison, human authority, or proof.
- General site crawling, generic WCAG reporting, multi-agent orchestration, and third-party auto-patching are excluded from the challenge build.
