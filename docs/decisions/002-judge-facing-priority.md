# ADR 002: Cross-route protection bypass is the judge-facing priority

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-08-26

## Context

Competitive review found strong WebMCP projects already demonstrating human approval, dynamic capability lifetime, tool inspection, execution replay, and exported evidence. Those mechanisms remain required for EqualTrace, but none is independently distinctive enough to lead the submission.

No reviewed project combined three real routes, semantic protection comparison, first-divergence diagnosis, human-approved repair, and proof by rerun.

## Decision

The judge-facing priority is:

> EqualTrace discovers when an agent reaches the correct final state while bypassing protections that constrained the equivalent human action.

The assistive route, dynamic repair capability, and parity receipt are evidence in the complete proof chain. They are not separate product headlines.

## Consequences

- The opening state must show the protection bypass before explaining the machinery.
- Three-route runtime capture and first divergence outrank generalized inspection features.
- Approval lifetime is implemented rigorously but positioned as the authority boundary for repair.
- A feature that does not strengthen the complete proof chain is deferred.
- Competitive claims describe the full chain rather than claiming that any individual mechanism is unique.
